# Occlusion realism check

Generated by `scripts/occlusion_realism.py` (seed 1337; edge_margin 0.1, descent_speed 0.3, min_visible_kp 4). Train clips only.

- Real hidden falls (OCCU, gap starting in a fall/fallen frame): 34 gaps, 94% classified hidden_down
- Simulated hidden falls (visible falls from GMDCSA24, caucafall, le2i, mcfd, of-syn): 352 gaps, 31% classified hidden_down
- Simulated occlusion during normal activity: 575 gaps, 4% classified hidden_down (lower is better: these would be false `hidden_down`)

`*_low_frames`: how many frames before the person vanished that body part's visibility was already below 0.5 (loss order: larger = lost earlier).

## Choosing descent_speed (body heights/s, real train clips)

Hip speed is measured in body heights/s (body height = the tallest the person stood in the last 2 s), because in frame heights/s the same fall reads 3x faster on a close camera than a far one (visible-fall speed 0.15 on MCFD vs 0.47 on GMDCSA24 in frame heights/s; 0.67-0.99 in body heights/s).

| descent_speed | occu_hidden_falls_caught | false_hidden_down_outside_falls | of_which_mcfd |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.15 | 94% | 40 | 35 |
| 0.2 | 94% | 34 | 30 |
| 0.3 | 94% | 24 | 22 |
| 0.4 | 88% | 17 | 16 |
| 0.5 | 82% | 9 | 9 |

## Status: accepted for now, revisit after E7

Decision (17 Oct 2026): the simulation is accepted as it is and judged by experiment E7, not by further tuning.

- Matches real OCCU: distance from the frame edge at loss, loss order (ankles first; hips and head visible until the person vanishes), and few false `hidden_down` during simulated normal activity.
- Known gap: simulated hidden falls vanish at a lower hip speed than real OCCU ones, so fewer of them are classified `hidden_down` (see the tables above). Simulated losses happen within about 0.3 s of the peak descent, so the gap is in the measured speed at loss, not the timing.
- Revisit if E7 shows no gain on OCCU val/test or the staged hidden-fall clips, or more false alerts elsewhere. Starting points: `RISE_EDGE_IN_DROP`, `MIN_DROP` and the `hips_hidden` loss rule in `src/fallsense/occlusion.py`.
